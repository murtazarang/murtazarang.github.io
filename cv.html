Research profile

My research investigates reusable algorithm implementations and high-throughput execution for single- and multi-agent reinforcement learning. I am the sole creator and principal architect of Coordination for Scale RL (casRL), which implements more than 100 algorithms and variants and sustains 2–4 million environment steps per second during end-to-end training on one CPU and one GPU. I also develop learning and coordination methods for heterogeneous robotic systems.
