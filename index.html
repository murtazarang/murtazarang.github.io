Hello, I'm {{ site.name }}

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at Virginia Tech, advised by R. K. Williams, and expect to graduate on December 16, 2026.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning, multi-agent reinforcement learning, and high-throughput research software for learning and robotics.

I created Coordination for Scale RL (casRL), an open-source PyTorch framework that builds RL and MARL experiments from reusable components and runs end-to-end training at 2–4 million environment steps per second on a single node with one CPU and one GPU.
